# Future weather layers: source and product guide

Research snapshot: 17 September 2026. This guide distinguishes shipped experimental layers from further candidates. Rain Alarm has a bounded Open-Meteo selected-point model/solar cache for Now indicators and a separate 5×5 **visible-viewport** wind-grid request used only when Wind is selected, after camera idle. It also has optional public EUMETView WMS accumulated-flash-area and nighttime fog/low-cloud layers. These are **not** per-flash detections, verified ground strikes, or confirmed surface fog. WMS rendering still needs physical-device acceptance. The older precipitation fallback in `OpenMeteoForecastRepository.kt` remains independent; future work could consolidate network/cache handling. The map shows a concise loading/available/unavailable state (or selected-place wind) while provider and licence attribution lives in Settings > About the data; source times remain internal for freshness safeguards. [Open-Meteo Forecast API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs), [EUMETView user guide](https://user.eumetsat.int/resources/user-guides/eumet-view-user-guide).

## Candidate features

| User request | Best initial source and meaning | Suitable UI / feasibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frequent local wind direction | Open-Meteo `current=wind_direction_10m,wind_speed_10m,wind_gusts_10m`, optionally `minutely_15` wind fields where supported. “Current” is based on **15-minute weather-model data**, not a live anemometer; outside Central Europe and North America, 15-minute values may be interpolated hourly data. Model runs refresh on source-specific schedules. [Forecast fields and caveat](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs), [model updates](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/model-updates). UK station measurements are a separate Met Office DataHub product, generally **hourly** and sparse, not a frequent per-user wind value. [Met Office DataHub](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/services/data/met-office-weather-datahub), [Land Observations details](https://datahub.metoffice.gov.uk/docs/g/category/observations/overview). | **Implemented experimental map layer:** 25 distinct Open-Meteo model points across the visible viewport after camera idle, cached by viewport for 15 minutes. A single selected-point speed/from-direction status beneath the current-location control is separate from the map grid. Large-scale atmospheric detail remains limited by the 5×5 sampling density. |
| Actual current lightning | EUMETSAT MTG Lightning Imager **Level-2 Lightning Flashes** (`EO:EUM:DAT:0691`) is operational near-real-time, with stated 4 km spatial resolution and free/unrestricted CC BY 4.0 policy. These are satellite-observed **flashes**, not verified cloud-to-ground strike coordinates. [LI Flashes catalogue](https://user.eumetsat.int/catalogue/EO%3AEUM%3ADAT%3A0691). EUMETSAT Data Store/API/EUMDAC access adds registration, ingestion and NetCDF/product parsing work; verify end-to-end latency before claiming “live”. [MTG data-access guide](https://user.eumetsat.int/resources/user-guides/mtg-data-access-guide). For ground-strike-type filtering, The Weather Company documents a global, limited-availability commercial recent-lightning API: 25 mi / 40 km local radius, 15-second to 15-minute windows, with query start within the past hour; subscription-specific limits require enquiry. [API specification](https://developer.weather.com/docs/openapi/recent-lightning-strikes-for-local-3-0-7). | **Implemented experimental WMS:** accumulated satellite flash **areas** (`mtg_fd:li_afa`). Individual satellite flash detections are feasible future work using the registered Data Store NetCDF collection, not available from this public WMS layer. **Do not** label Open-Meteo's `lightning_potential` model variable as observed flashes or strikes. [Forecast variable list](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs). |
| Sunrise / sunset | Open-Meteo daily `sunrise,sunset` at the selected coordinate and timezone. They are daily astronomical times, not a continuously refreshed meteorological observation. [Daily fields](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs). | **Implemented:** selected-place 24-hour sunrise/sunset readout in the Now card, formatted in the returned IANA timezone and refreshed after local-day rollover. A map day/night terminator would need separate geometry; no raster weather overlay is necessary. |
| UV | Open-Meteo hourly `uv_index` and daily `uv_index_max`/`uv_index_clear_sky_max` style variables as documented; label forecast/model and distinguish clear-sky from all-sky. [Forecast fields](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs). | Point card with source/valid time and carefully explained risk band. A heat-map needs gridded input, not one point value painted over the viewport. |
| Temperature, pressure, humidity | Open-Meteo `current` and hourly `temperature_2m`, `relative_humidity_2m`, `pressure_msl` or `surface_pressure`, plus optional dewpoint. Current is model-based 15-minute data. [Forecast fields and current semantics](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs). Met Office DataHub offers UK hourly instrument observations at station points, with station spacing/coverage caveats. [DataHub](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/services/data/met-office-weather-datahub), [observations](https://datahub.metoffice.gov.uk/docs/g/category/observations/overview). | **Implemented experimental Now indicators** for temperature, pressure, humidity and UV, each independently hideable; selected-point Wind is also opt-in. Station comparison/spatial contours remain future work. |
| Fog | Open-Meteo `weather_code` (45/48), `visibility`, low cloud cover and humidity can indicate **modeled fog potential or low visibility**, not a high-resolution observed fog boundary. [Forecast variables](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs). EUMETSAT's operational near-real-time MTG Fog / Low Clouds RGB (`EO:EUM:DAT:1023`, stated 1 km visualisation, CC BY 4.0) is a more credible spatial candidate, but it identifies low water cloud/fog together. [Product catalogue](https://user.eumetsat.int/catalogue/EO%3AEUM%3ADAT%3A1023). The related Night Microphysics guide says it is night-only, can miss thin or cirrus-obscured fog and cannot distinguish surface fog from low cloud with certainty. [Quick guide](https://user.eumetsat.int/resources/user-guides/night-microphysics-rgb-quick-guide), [EUMETSAT example](https://user.eumetsat.int/resources/case-studies/fog-or-low-cloud). | **Implemented experimental nighttime WMS** `mtg_fd:rgb_fog`, withheld by day/stale/unknown daylight. It remains fog **or low cloud**, not a surface-fog diagnosis. |

## Delivery rules and budgets

1. **Point data before map data.** The shipped selected-place point request now includes current temperature, pressure, humidity, UV, daylight and wind together with daily sunrise/sunset, parsed and cached with source times. Reuse it for additional Now facts rather than making another call merely to display temperature. The older precipitation-fallback client is still separate. [Open-Meteo request field definitions](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs).
2. **Respect source cadence, not animation cadence.** The point cache refreshes on selected-place change, manual refresh, 15-minute age and local-day rollover; the wind grid is requested only after a meaningful settled viewport change or refresh. These are app policies, **not** provider-promised update intervals: individual models have different publication schedules and current values are model-derived. [Open-Meteo model updates](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/model-updates), [current semantics](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs). Reuse a single request for several fields/places where API and license allow; size requests and avoid per-frame fetches.
3. **Budget before enabling an overlay.** Open-Meteo's free API is noncommercial-only with stated limits below 10,000 requests/day, 5,000/hour and 600/minute, CC BY 4.0 attribution and no availability guarantee. Check current terms and request-count rules, including large variable/location requests, before shipping. [Terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms), [pricing/request accounting](https://open-meteo.com/en/pricing). UK Met Office DataHub and commercial lightning have their own access/contract conditions; do not assume the Rain Alarm open-source license grants data redistribution rights. [DataHub](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/services/data/met-office-weather-datahub), [commercial lightning API](https://developer.weather.com/docs/openapi/recent-lightning-strikes-for-local-3-0-7).
4. **Spatial layers need spatial data.** Use actual georeferenced product grids/point detections, explicit projection and bounded visible-region cache. Carry observation/acquisition time and uncertainty through to rendering. Do not make a full map raster by repeating a single point forecast. Satellite/NRT files may require a small backend/transcoding service; assess user registration, licensing, bandwidth and operating costs first. [EUMETSAT MTG access](https://user.eumetsat.int/resources/user-guides/mtg-data-access-guide).
5. **Privacy and safety.** Ask no additional location permission merely for point data; use the existing selected saved/live place and never persist virtual live coordinates without a product decision. Minimize third-party requests, avoid embedding commercial API keys in an APK, keep attribution in the dedicated data-information UI, and retain source time internally for freshness decisions. Lightning and fog are especially safety-sensitive: no “all clear” claim from an empty, delayed or unavailable feed. EUMETSAT flash data are satellite flashes; Weather Company's strike type includes cloud-to-cloud and cloud-to-ground distinctions. [LI catalogue](https://user.eumetsat.int/catalogue/EO%3AEUM%3ADAT%3A0691), [Weather Company API](https://developer.weather.com/docs/openapi/recent-lightning-strikes-for-local-3-0-7).

## Recommended sequence for remaining work

1. Consolidate the current-weather point cache with the older precipitation-fallback client where practical. Sunrise/sunset are now present; verify Now readout spacing, timezone, units and map wind arrows on a real device. [Forecast API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs).
2. Evaluate optional UK hourly station observations as a **comparison**, only after checking DataHub account, terms, coverage and per-station availability. Do not relabel station data as the exact selected-point condition. [Met Office DataHub](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/services/data/met-office-weather-datahub).
3. Assess individual EUMETSAT LFL flash detections as a separate research/service adapter with registered access, licence/attribution, latency, NetCDF parsing, device bandwidth and map-marker density measured. The public AFA WMS already shows flash activity areas, but cannot provide event points. Defer commercial ground-strike feed pending license/cost decision. [LI Flashes](https://user.eumetsat.int/catalogue/EO%3AEUM%3ADAT%3A0691), [access guide](https://user.eumetsat.int/resources/user-guides/mtg-data-access-guide), [commercial alternative](https://developer.weather.com/docs/openapi/recent-lightning-strikes-for-local-3-0-7).
4. Verify the experimental nighttime fog/low-cloud WMS on a physical device and with daytime/nighttime transitions; consider a clearly labelled point modeled-visibility chip separately. [MTG fog product](https://user.eumetsat.int/catalogue/EO%3AEUM%3ADAT%3A1023), [Night Microphysics limitations](https://user.eumetsat.int/resources/user-guides/night-microphysics-rgb-quick-guide).
