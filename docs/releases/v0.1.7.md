# Rain Alarm 0.1.7

This release makes the open RainViewer source more consistent across Radar, Now and notifications, adds optional likely-snow presentation, and fixes map positioning during selection and zoom changes.

- RainViewer Universal Blue RGB is decoded onto Rain Alarm's established Light/Medium/Severe intensity scale before map rendering and point analysis. Source smoothing, temporal interpolation and the regional palette treatment remain intact.
- Settings shows **Show likely snow** when RainViewer is explicitly selected. Likely snow uses an icy lavender-to-indigo map/graph palette, snow wording in Now and notifications, and the supplied snowflake texture on the compass centre and direction pointer. It remains off for automatic RainViewer fallback.
- A fresh RainViewer tile with no above-threshold echoes can now produce a flat, clear next-hour Now series without requiring a motion vector—but only when RainViewer's separate coverage mask verifies the selected point and substantially the complete retained tile. Missing, stale, invalid or out-of-coverage data remains unavailable rather than becoming a false dry result.
- When echoes exist, the local dense motion field remains preferred. A confidence-gated broad translation can provide a cautious arrival estimate when the dense field is unavailable; if neither is reliable, Now and alerts remain unavailable. Both paths stop at the downloaded edge instead of padding unseen space as dry, and the alert worker consumes the same minute series as Now.
- Selecting or long-press-saving a different place now centres that place at the retained zoom. Refreshing or re-entering the same selection preserves its exact pan and zoom. Radar overlays are reprojected once more at camera idle so provider pixels remain geographically fixed after a pan, zoom or detail-tier transition.

MeteoGroup and RainViewer remain independent composites and can materially disagree about precipitation footprint, intensity and timing. Rain Alarm calibrates their presentation; it does not expand, shrink or move one provider's echoes to imitate the other. RainViewer supplies observations only, so its future positions and arrival times are local estimates and can legitimately be unavailable.

## Install or upgrade

Install the signed `RainAlarm-v0.1.7-release.apk` on Android 8.0 or later. Version code 8 and the unchanged permanent release certificate allow an in-place update from signed v0.1.0–v0.1.6 while preserving local settings. A debug-signed APK has a different certificate and cannot update an official release in place; uninstalling it may remove local data.

Rain Alarm is an experimental weather aid, not a safety-critical warning service. Radar resolution, coverage and freshness vary by provider; normalized severity is not rainfall or snowfall rate. Likely-snow classification is model-assisted and does not confirm surface snowfall. Notification checks are scheduled by Android and may be delayed by Doze, connectivity or provider availability. Current location uses foreground fixes only. See the [README](https://github.com/Undert0e-505/RainAlarm#readme) for attribution, privacy and other limits.
