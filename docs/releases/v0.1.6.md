# Rain Alarm 0.1.6

This update hardens Radar refresh rendering and makes repeated rain notifications expire predictably. Radar sources, rain classification, forecast calculations and map controls are otherwise unchanged.

- Dark-map refresh no longer reveals a partial/default MapLibre frame during a session handoff. The theme-matched cover waits for a complete frame from the requested style, preventing the intermittent pale flash seen during refresh.
- A sent rain notification now covers the forecast wet interval recorded at send time, through its inclusive final wet minute plus a 10-minute grace period. Forecast refreshes during that interval remain suppressed without moving the expiry.
- Once that absolute expiry passes, a later qualifying approach for the same place can alert again without requiring an intervening full-clear forecast. It replaces that place's existing notification card and alerts again rather than accumulating cards or silently updating.
- Deleting a saved place clears its persisted alert record and dismisses its visible rain notification without disturbing other places or virtual Current location.
- Existing installs with a legacy stuck/disarmed record receive a finite migration expiry based on the previous notification time, so the place cannot remain permanently silent.

## Install or upgrade

Install the signed `RainAlarm-v0.1.6-release.apk` on Android 8.0 or later. Version code 7 and the unchanged release certificate allow an in-place update from signed v0.1.0–v0.1.5 while preserving local settings. A debug-signed APK has a different certificate and cannot update an official release in place; uninstalling it may remove local data.

Rain Alarm is an experimental weather aid, not a safety-critical warning service. Notification checks are scheduled by Android and may be delayed by Doze, connectivity or provider availability. A suppression expiry does not imply the forecast was correct, and Android notification-channel settings can independently silence alerts. Current location uses foreground fixes only. Wind is model-derived, Lightning shows satellite flash **areas** rather than individual ground strikes, and Fog shows nighttime fog/low-cloud imagery rather than confirmed surface fog. See the [README](https://github.com/Undert0e-505/RainAlarm#readme) for data sources, attribution, privacy and other limits.
