# Rain Alarm 0.9.0

Rain Alarm 0.9.0 is the first feature-complete preview on the road to 1.0. It brings the app's
place-specific rain answer to the home screen and makes Radar useful while travelling.

## Independent home-screen widgets

- Configure every widget for one saved place, independently of the place currently open in the
  app.
- Compact and resizable layouts show rain arrival or current rain, qualitative intensity,
  direction, likely snow where supported, nearby lightning and temperature when dry.
- Wider layouts add available stop/window detail and a next-hour graph. Per-widget opacity and
  quiet hours fit the widget to its launcher and notification needs.
- Widgets refresh without opening Rain Alarm, subject to Android's inexact WorkManager, Doze,
  force-stop and manufacturer battery policies.

## Travel with automatic current-time Radar

- One tap enters Travel, follows the foreground device position and recentres on demand without
  changing the startup place.
- **AUTO** keeps the Radar timeline on the exact device clock and advances to the provider
  prediction or estimate valid for now while enabled data checks follow source publication times.
- Scrubbing or Play takes manual time control; pressing Travel again recentres and restores AUTO.
  Position-update cadence and visual smoothness remain dependent on Android, the phone and its
  GNSS hardware, and are a planned 1.0 refinement.

## Lightning activity integration

- An optional notification reports EUMETSAT observed accumulated flash-area activity within
  15 km of the monitored place. This is not a strike forecast or safety warning.
- Widgets can show nearby Lightning independently of the app's persistent Radar layer setting.
- When rain and Lightning become alert-eligible together, Rain Alarm sends one combined
  notification. Tapping a Lightning notification or widget opens its exact Radar place with
  Lightning temporarily enabled for ten minutes.

## Also included

Multi-provider Radar with honest coverage masks and optional Wind/Clouds layers; improved Places;
manual and automatic day/night appearance profiles; a contextual first-use tour; and installed
English, Dutch, Belgian Dutch, German, French, Welsh and Irish localization.

## Install or update

Download and open `RainAlarm-v0.9.0-release.apk`. Android may ask you to allow installation from the
app used to open the file.

This release supports Android 8.0+ and has version code **20**. It uses Rain Alarm's unchanged
permanent release certificate, so it can update an earlier official release-signed installation in
place while retaining local places and settings. A debug-signed build cannot be updated in place by
this APK.

Rain Alarm remains an experimental information aid. Radar, widgets and notifications depend on
third-party coverage, freshness, network access and Android scheduling; no dry state or absent
Lightning detection is a safety assurance. See the [user guide](../USER_GUIDE.md),
[data-source notes](../DATA_SOURCES.md) and [privacy disclosure](../PRIVACY.md).
